# Y.O.U.R. Experience · v2.4 · Living District city + Morrow City

Play: open `index.html` (it starts the Living District, the main Y.O.U.R. city). Everything runs offline in the browser; there is no server code.

- **Living District:** a living superhero city (CCE 2.0). Civilians with routines, five independent supers, Dr. Iteron's robots, Vault Vesper's heist week,
  and The Divider, a hero manipulator who frames heroes, stages fights between them and sways public opinion. Mella can walk, skate, drive, fight, talk and
  calm. Veyru lives at home and decides whether to come along (KAW Living Brain).
- **Morrow City:** a district of the city, reached through the Morrow Line gate by the Transit Depot or Menu → Travel to Morrow City (METAL v17 engine,
  Metal Story missions 1–10, METAL as an ally).
- **Valence day:** the earlier v2.0–v2.3 day systems, at `index.html?valence=1` or Menu → Play the Valence day.

Controls: WASD/arrows move · J hit · L blast · K dash · Space jump · E talk/act · C calm · Q skate · F car · Esc menu · H history. Gamepad and touch work too.

All characters, villains and places are original WIR / Y.O.U.R. characters. Combat uses the Nimbus module `your/v23/vendor/wir_mella_combat.js` unchanged.
